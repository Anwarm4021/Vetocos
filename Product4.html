Munisan

Complementary feed for dogs and cats
Supports natural defenses using beta-glucans and HMB to help stimulate the immune system.

Instructions for use:

Oral administration:
- Dogs and cats up to 10 kg: 1 capsule per day
- Dogs over 10 kg: 2 capsules per day
Use for 2 weeks to a maximum of 1 month. The Twist Off capsule allows for easy opening: twist or cut the tip and apply the contents on the tongue, paw, or food. Follow veterinary guidance for dose and duration.

Always ensure fresh water is available. Keep out of reach of children and animals.

Composition (per capsule):

  • Beta-(1,3/1,6)-glucan – 80 mg
  • Calcium salts of beta-hydroxy-beta-methylbutyric acid (HMB-Ca) – 25 mg
  • Valerian root extract (Valeriana officinalis L.) – 5.22 mg

Packaging: Bottle of 15 or 60 Twist Off capsules
