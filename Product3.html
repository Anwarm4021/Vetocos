NeuroView

Complementary feed for dogs and cats
Provides essential nutrients to support ocular, cerebral, and nervous system functions. Rich in protective antioxidants.

Instructions for use:

Oral administration:
- Dogs and cats up to 10 kg: 1 capsule per day
- Over 10 kg: 1 capsule per 10 kg per day
The Twist Off capsule allows easy use: twist or cut the tip, then administer directly or mix with food. Dosage and duration may be adjusted by the veterinarian.

Ensure access to fresh water at all times. Keep out of reach of children and animals.

Composition (per capsule):

  • Fish oil (DHA) – 64 mg
  • Marigold flower extract (source of lutein & zeaxanthin) – 21.5 mg
  • Acerola (source of Vitamin C: 35 mg) – 50 mg
  • Vitamin E – 22.4 mg
  • Beta-carotene – 9 mg
  • Taurine – 4.5 mg
  • Vitamin A – 0.2 mg
  • Vitamin B1 – 0.01 mg
  • Vitamin B6 – 0.09 mg
  • Calcium pantothenate (Vitamin B5) – 1 mg
  • Niacinamide (Vitamin B3) – 1 mg
  • Folic acid (Vitamin B9) – 0.02 mg
  • Biotin (Vitamin B8) – 0.009 mg
  • Vitamin B12 – 0.002 mg
  • Zinc – 2.5 mg
  • Selenium – 0.4 µg
  • Grape extract (source of flavonoids) – 53.8 mg
  • Curcuma rhizome extract – 31.4 mg
  • Green tea leaf extract (source of polyphenols) – 32.6 mg
