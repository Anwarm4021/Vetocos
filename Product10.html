Carbo Digest

Complementary feed for dogs and cats
Designed to protect the intestinal lining, capture gases, toxins, and water, and support natural defenses by modulating gut flora.

Instructions for use:

Oral administration: Mix with food for 5 to 10 days.

  • Up to 7 kg: ½ scoop (soft stool), 1 scoop (liquid stool)
  • 7–15 kg: 1 scoop (soft), 2 scoops (liquid)
  • 15–30 kg: 2 scoops (soft), 4 scoops (liquid)
  • Over 30 kg: ½ scoop per 10 kg (soft), 1 scoop per 10 kg (liquid)

1 scoop = 5 g. Follow veterinary recommendations. Ensure access to fresh water. Keep out of reach of children and animals.

Composition:

  • Charcoal (activated)
  • Yeast cell walls (MOS)
