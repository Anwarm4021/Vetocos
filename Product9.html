Art-ASUH

Complementary feed for dogs and cats
Supports joint flexibility and cartilage resilience. Enhances comfort, suppleness, and mobility in animals with joint stress or aging-related issues.

Instructions for use:

Oral administration:

  • Art-ASUH S (Twist Off, small size): 1 capsule per day for every 5 kg body weight for 3 weeks to several months. Twist to open and administer directly or with food.
  • Art-ASUH XL (tablet, large size): 1 tablet per 20 kg body weight per day for 3 weeks to several months. Tablets can be crushed and mixed with food. For multiple tablets, split across 2–3 doses daily.

Veterinary adjustment of dosage and duration is advised. Provide constant access to fresh water. Keep out of reach of children and animals.

Composition:

  • Glucosamine: 169 mg (S), 300 mg (XL)
  • Chondroitin sulfate: 22.23 mg (S), 30 mg (XL)
  • Hyaluronic acid: 5 mg (S), 7 mg (XL)
  • Avocado extracts: 33.33 mg (S), 145 mg (XL)
  • Soy oil: 453 mg (S)
  • Soy lecithin: 46.33 mg (S)
  • Unsaponifiable avocado/soy fraction: 14.8 mg (S), 30.8 mg (XL)
  • L-Carnitine: 24.3 mg (S)
  • Vitamin C: 25 mg (both)
  • Manganese: 2.24 mg (S), 1.05 mg (XL)
  • Zinc: 1 mg
  • Copper: 1 mg
  • Harpagophytum procumbens: 33.33 mg (S), 10 mg (XL)
