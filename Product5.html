Pilosan

Complementary feed for dogs and cats
Supports a healthy skin balance and helps restore skin and coat integrity. Enriched with essential fatty acids and vitamins to nourish and protect.

Instructions for use:

Oral administration:

  • Dogs and cats up to 15 kg: 1 capsule per day
  • Dogs over 15 kg: 2 capsules per day

Recommended duration: 3 weeks to several months. Twist Off capsule format ensures easy administration—twist or cut to open and give directly or mix with food. Dosage may be adjusted by a veterinarian.

Provide access to fresh water. Keep out of reach of children and animals.

Composition (per capsule):

  • Refined borage oil (omega-6 source)
  • Fish oil (omega-3 source)
  • Lecithin
  • Zinc – 8 mg
  • Vitamin B12 – 5 μg
  • D-Biotin – 1 mg
  • Vitamin B2 – 0.5 mg
  • Vitamin B6 – 0.2 mg
  • Vitamin B1 – 0.2 mg
  • Omega-3 (EPA + DHA) – 41.76 mg
  • Omega-6 (GLA + LA) – 75.9 mg

Packaging: Bottle of 20, 60, or 90 Twist Off capsules
