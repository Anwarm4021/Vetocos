Hepatiale

Complementary feed for dogs and cats
Supports optimal liver function using a blend of phosphatidylcholine and L-aspartic acid.

Instructions for use:

Hepatiale S (Twist Off):
- For cats: 1 capsule per day
- For small dogs: 1 capsule per 5 kg per day
Administer for 1 to 3 months. Capsule can be opened and content given directly or mixed with food.

Hepatiale M:
- 1 tablet per 15 kg of body weight per day for 1 to 3 months.

Hepatiale XL:
- 1 tablet per 25 kg of body weight per day for 1 to 3 months.

Tablets can be crushed and mixed with food. If multiple tablets are needed daily, divide into 2 or 3 doses. Follow veterinary recommendations.

Composition (per dose):

  • Hepatiale S: Phosphatidylcholine 85 mg, L-aspartic acid 85 mg
  • Hepatiale M: Phosphatidylcholine 150 mg, L-aspartic acid 150 mg
  • Hepatiale XL: Phosphatidylcholine 275 mg, L-aspartic acid 275 mg

Packaging: S (10/40 caps), M (10/30 tablets), XL (10/40 tablets)

Price: 135 MAD (varies by size and supplier)
