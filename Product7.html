Urocline Stones

Complementary feed for cats
Acidifying formulation designed to support urinary comfort. Palatable format ensures easy administration without stress.

Instructions for use:

Oral administration:
- Cats: 2 capsules per day (1 in the morning, 1 in the evening)
Recommended for 3 to 6 weeks. Use Twist Off capsules by twisting or cutting the tip. Content can be placed directly in the mouth, on the paw, or mixed with food.

Dosage and duration may be modified upon veterinary recommendation. Provide fresh water at all times. Keep out of reach of children and animals.

Composition (per capsule):

  • Soybean oil
  • Soy lecithin
  • Glucosamine – 21 mg
  • Cranberry extract – 31 mg
  • DL-methionine – 250 mg
  • Vitamin C (L-ascorbic acid) – 75 mg
  • Vitamin B6 (Pyridoxine) – 3 mg
  • Parsley extract – 12.5 mg
  • Valerian extract – 10 mg
  • Nettle leaf extract – 10 mg
