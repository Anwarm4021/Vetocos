Oxyfer

Complementary feed for dogs and cats
Designed to support oxygen transport and red blood cell formation. Ideal during anemia, post-surgery recovery, and periods of fatigue or physical exertion.

Instructions for use:

Oral administration: 1 tablet per 10 kg of body weight daily for 2 to 4 weeks. Tablets can be crushed and mixed with food. For multiple tablets, divide into 2 or 3 doses.

Follow veterinary recommendations. Provide access to clean water at all times. Store in a cool, dry place. Keep out of reach of children and pets.

Composition (per tablet):

  • Iron (iron gluconate) – 50 mg
  • Vitamin B12 – 0.05 mg
  • Vitamin C – 30 mg
  • Folic acid – 0.2 mg
  • Vitamin B6 – 1.5 mg
  • Copper – 1 mg
