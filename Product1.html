Urocline Chien

Complementary feed for dogs
Supports healthy urinary function and is beneficial in cases of urinary discomfort.

Instructions for use:

Oral administration. 1 tablet per 10 kg of body weight daily for 1 to 3 weeks. Dosage and duration may vary based on veterinary advice. Tablets can be crushed and mixed with food. If multiple tablets are required per day, they can be split into 2 or 3 doses.

Ensure the dog has access to fresh water at all times. Keep out of reach of children and animals.

Composition (per tablet):

  • Vaccinium macrocarpon (Cranberry extract) – 300 mg
  • Glucosamine – 50 mg
  • Petroselinum sativum (Parsley extract) – 52 mg

Packaging: Bottle of 14 or 30 tablets
