Qalmil

Complementary feed for dogs and cats
Designed to promote calm and relaxation, Qalmil combines chamomile, valerian, and tryptophan in a palatable Twist Off capsule format for stress-prone animals.

Instructions for use:

Oral administration:

  • Dogs and cats up to 10 kg: 1 capsule per day
  • Dogs 10–25 kg: 2 capsules per day
  • Dogs over 25 kg: 3 capsules per day

Recommended duration: 1 week to several months depending on need. Twist Off capsule can be opened by twisting or cutting the tip. Content can be given directly or mixed with food, placed on the paw or tongue. Dosage may be adjusted by the veterinarian.

Always ensure access to fresh water. Keep out of reach of children and animals.

Composition (per capsule):

  • Lecithin
  • L-Tryptophan – 50 mg
  • Chamomile extract (Chamomilla recutita) – 80 mg
  • Valerian extract (Valeriana officinalis) – 20 mg

Packaging: Bottle of 14 or 60 Twist Off capsules
