HemoVet

Complementary feed for dogs and cats
Supports red blood cell formation and overall vitality, especially during anemia or recovery phases.

Instructions for use:

Oral administration:
- Cats and dogs: 1 capsule per 10 kg of body weight daily
Use for 1 to 3 weeks or as advised by your veterinarian. Twist Off capsules can be opened and the content mixed with food or administered directly.

Ensure access to clean drinking water. Store in a dry, cool place. Keep out of reach of children and pets.

Composition (per capsule):

  • Iron (ferrous fumarate) – 20 mg
  • Vitamin B12 – 0.025 mg
  • Folic acid – 0.2 mg
  • Vitamin B6 – 1 mg
  • Vitamin C – 25 mg

Packaging: Bottle of 30 Twist Off capsules
